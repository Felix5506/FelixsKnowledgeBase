# Recipe-Finder-App

## Languages to use
* TypeScript
* HTML/CSS
* Javascript

## Summary of Idea
A general use app for determining what a user can cook based on a list of ingredients put into the app. Ideally, it could fill in missing ingredients and display nutritional information and cost of anything the user can cook. This would likely be a web app, although I may intend to port it to mobile devices in the future.
