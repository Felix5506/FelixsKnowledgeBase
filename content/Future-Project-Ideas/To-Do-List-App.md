# To-Do-List-App

## Languages to use
* Dart
* Flutter

## Summary of Idea
This idea was suggested by a friend, to create a basic to do list app with the gimmick of accumulating points from tasks completed to give yourself a reward at the end. Essentially it just provides extra motivation to complete your daily tasks on top of what typical to do list apps do.
